# Marketing One Page Studio

Produce one decisive page, not a brainstorm dump. Make every block usable in a website form, document, or AI prompt field.

## Intake

Capture or safely infer:

- Product and confirmed truth.
- Audience, market, and objective.
- Offer, proof, and desired action.
- Channels and image provider.
- Brand cues and anti-references.
- Product, identity, text, and claim locks.

Ask at most three questions only when a missing answer changes product truth, rights, identity, or campaign architecture. Label all assumptions.

## Build three lanes

1. **Clear**: fastest comprehension and strongest proof.
2. **Signature**: one ownable visual grammar tied to product mechanism.
3. **Departure**: one deliberate category rule break with a stated risk.

Recommend one lane. Do not create lanes that differ only by color or adjectives.

## Return exactly these seven blocks

### 1. Truth and Assumptions

Separate confirmed, inferred, and unknown facts. Never turn unknown information into packaging text, product claims, reviews, statistics, or proof.

### 2. Campaign Core

State the audience tension, desired belief, campaign statement, message ladder, proof mechanism, CTA, and recommended lane.

### 3. Creative Lanes

For each lane give the idea, hero composition, visual grammar, copy line, best channel, and risk.

### 4. Asset Plan

List the minimum useful assets by channel and ratio. Recompose `9:16`, `4:5`, `1:1`, and wide web masters instead of blindly cropping.

### 5. Final Image Prompt

Use labeled sections: Job, References and Locks, Subject and Action, Scene, Composition, Camera and Light, Materials, Copy Space, and Do Not.

For generated human imagery with no conflicting direction, begin exactly:

```text
Create a completely RAW quality, unprocessed, unedited image with full iPhone camera quality.
```

Then default to a fictional adult, Korean K-pop-inspired makeup, natural skin texture, and a plausible slender healthy idol-like silhouette. Never impose these defaults on an existing real person during editing.

### 6. One-Page Website

Define a mobile-first sequence: hero promise, immediate proof, tension, product mechanism, use or demonstration, trust, offer, FAQ if needed, and final action. Vary section grammar; avoid equal card grids, generic AI gradients, glass dashboards, and decorative filler.

### 7. QA and Export

Check product and identity fidelity, anatomy, physics, claims, rights, hierarchy, safe zones, and channel fit. Return a pre-flight score with blockers and warnings, then a compact JSON payload when the user wants website integration.

Before emitting JSON, read `assets/one-page-schema.json` and use its exact field names and required shapes. Do not rename `hero` to `hero_composition`, `headline` to `copy_line`, or asset production fields to looser alternatives.

## Hard rules

- Default generated people to adults and plausible anatomy.
- Require a packshot for exact packaging fidelity; otherwise label concept art and omit readable microtext.
- Preserve real-person identity, age presentation, skin tone, face, and body unless a specific authorized edit says otherwise.
- Never invent claims, testimonials, labels, statistics, certifications, or endorsements.
- Never substitute purple-blue fog, random orbs, plastic skin, petals, splashes, marble, fake dashboards, or equal cards for a campaign idea.
- State when an image was not rendered or a result was not inspected.
