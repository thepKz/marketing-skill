# PR and Earned Communications

This file has two halves that work differently. The craft sections are judgement: whether an
announcement is news, what a narrative owes a journalist, what belongs in the pack. The sections from
`## What the law attaches to a placement` onward are not judgement, because Vietnamese advertising law
reaches a placement whether or not anyone in the room was thinking of it as advertising. Read that part
before agreeing a media plan, and read it again before signing a partnership.

## Newsworthiness gate

Do not write a press release until the announcement has a defensible reason for media or a specific audience to care. Score:

- Newness or material change.
- Audience impact.
- Timeliness or cultural relevance.
- Credible evidence.
- Human, market, local, or industry consequence.
- Availability of an authorized spokesperson and useful media assets.

If weak, recommend owned content, customer communication, a data story, expert commentary, or a smaller targeted pitch instead of pretending everything is news.

## Narrative contract

Define:

- Confirmed announcement and date.
- Primary audience and journalist/editor beat.
- One news angle and one supporting angle.
- Why now.
- Evidence, numbers, customer examples, or market context with sources.
- Authorized quotes; never invent a quote.
- Availability, geography, price, terms, and embargo only when confirmed.

## Artifact pack

Produce only what the route needs:

- Press release or newsroom announcement.
- Short media pitch personalized by beat.
- Press fact sheet.
- Company/product boilerplate.
- Spokesperson Q&A and difficult questions.
- Press-ready hero, product editorial image, founder portrait, logo files, captions, credits, and usage terms.
- Data chart or methodology note when the story depends on research.
- Press page structure and contact information placeholder.

## Media outreach

Research current journalists and outlets by actual recent coverage; do not use stale lists or scrape private contact data. Record beat fit, relevant article, pitch rationale, outreach status, response, and follow-up. Do not mass-send identical pitches.

## Issues and crisis guardrails

For safety incidents, legal disputes, layoffs, security events, health claims, or active crises: distinguish confirmed facts, unknowns, actions taken, owner, next update, and legal/communications approval. Do not speculate, minimize harm, blame users, or fabricate resolution.

The crisis plan also needs the removal owner named below. A plan that can draft a holding statement but
cannot pull a placement inside a day is missing the part with a fine attached.

Measure relevant pickups, quality/authority, backlinks, referral traffic, share of voice, message pull-through, qualified inquiries, and downstream action. Do not value raw mention count equally.

## What the law attaches to a placement

Every band and article below sits in `data/vn-advertising-law.csv` with the instrument, the gazette URL
and the day it was read. Never quote a figure from this page without opening that row. A band
remembered from prose is how a wrong number reaches a client.

### The 24-hour clock is an operations problem

Nghị định 342/2025 Điều 18.2 puts one duty on the advertiser, the ad-service business, the publisher
and the conveyor alike: block or remove unlawful advertising, or cooperate in its removal, within 24
hours of a request from Bộ Văn hóa, Thể thao và Du lịch or a sector authority. The request counts if it
arrives `qua phương tiện điện tử`. Email starts the clock.

Read that against the notice clock in `lifecycle-retention.md`. Điều 42 of the consumer law counts
working days, so it pauses over a weekend. This one counts hours and never mentions business hours.

A request landing at six on Friday evening expires at six on Saturday. Nothing pauses.

Missing it costs more than the fine does. Điều 18.3 puts Bộ Văn hóa, Thể thao và Du lịch and Bộ Công an
on to technical blocking of the advertising or the service, and the block lifts only once the offending
advertising has been dealt with as required. So a brand that leaves an email unread for two days can be
blocked mid-campaign, then wait in someone else's queue to be unblocked.

The fine stacks. Điều 56.4 of Nghị định 87/2026 prices the miss at 50 to 60 million, and Điều 56.3.d
prices a refusal at 40 to 50 million, while the row `open-which-takedown-limb-applies` records that the
text does not settle which limb takes a given case. Both double for an organisation under Điều 6.

Điều 18.1 removes the last excuse for treating the request as an opening position. Where sector law is
silent or the poster cannot be identified, the ministry is the lead body, and a referring ministry
supplies the competent authority's conclusion along with its report. That conclusion was reached before
the email arrived. On content said to touch national security the wording tightens again, to
`kịp thời` with the 24 hours as a ceiling rather than an allowance.

So the deliverable is not a paragraph in a policy document. Name the owner. Name who covers evenings,
weekends and the Tết week, then write down the address the request will arrive at and who reads it that
day. An escalation chain that routes this through two approvals has already missed the window.

### The counterparty's legal status is your exposure

Điều 56.3.c prices advertising cooperation with a party that a competent authority has publicly
announced as violating the law at 40 to 50 million, and the announcement is the trigger. Check it
before signing. The limb reaches accounts, content channels, community pages and groups as well as
organisations and sites, which covers most of what a media or creator list actually contains.

Điều 56.5.b is where it turns expensive. Forced surrender of illegal profits attaches to Điều 56.3.b
and Điều 56.3.c, and not to the adjacency limb at Điều 56.3.a. Adjacency and cooperation carry the same
40 to 50 million band, so the whole difference lives in the remedy: the law prices who you chose to work
with above where a bought placement happened to land. Read the remedy.

Two things follow for the paperwork. A partnership agreement needs a representation on current status
and a right to stop on naming, because Điều 56.3.c gives no grace period for a contract signed before
the announcement. And Điều 56.3.đ makes being unable to name the parties behind a placement an offence
at the same band. Keep the brief.

### Print, which nothing else in this skill covers

A front page cannot lawfully be bought. Điều 57.2.a prices advertising on `bìa một` of a magazine or
`trang nhất` of a newspaper at 10 to 15 million, with no permitted version and no waiver in the
article. Where an outlet has offered one on a rate card, the offer is itself the problem.

The area cap sits in the Law rather than in the penalty decree. Luật 75/2025 Điều 21.1 as amended lets
advertising fill 30 percent of one newspaper issue or 40 percent of one magazine issue, excluding
advertising-only titles and advertising supplements, and Điều 57.3.a prices the excess at 15 to 20
million. It measures the issue. So a brand buying the last page available cannot tell from its own
booking whether the issue has gone over.

The rest of the article catches anyone who assumes print is the unregulated half. Advertising on a
`bản tin` is prohibited outright, at 3 to 5 million under Điều 57.1.c. Điều 57.3.b requires page one of
an advertising supplement to carry the paper's name, the press agency's name and address, and the line
`Phụ trương quảng cáo không tính vào giá bán`, at 15 to 20 million — one of the few places in this
corpus where the wording itself is prescribed.

Now the asymmetry. Điều 57.2.b prices an unmarked print advertisement at 10 to 15 million, against 30
to 40 million for the identical editorial failure online under Điều 56.2.a. The same mistake costs
three times as much on a network.

### Where the boundary actually falls

The question a PR person wants answered is whether earned coverage is advertising. This corpus does not
answer it. What it does is take away both of the easy answers.

The row `advertising-defined` records the amended Điều 2.1: using people or means to introduce a
product, or the organisation trading it, to the person receiving the introduction. No payment appears
in that definition and no profit purpose. So "we did not pay for it" is not an argument the definition
supports.

The opposite is no better founded, because the scope provision of the unamended Luật 16/2012
that might carve out news reporting is not in this corpus, so "it is journalism" is unsupported here
too. Both fail.

What is settled is that the outlet sits inside the regime. Điều 2.7 as amended names `cơ quan báo chí`
in the definition of `người phát hành quảng cáo`, beside publishers and site owners, and Điều 23.3
makes liability concurrent across advertiser, agency, publisher and conveyor. So the marker duty and
the front-page prohibition are the outlet's problem as much as yours.

Treat an advertorial as advertising. Mark it. Argue the other way only on a lawyer's letter.

The disclosure duty itself is not restated here. It is in `creator-ugc.md` for a creator's post, and in
`claims-proof-ledger.md` for what a claim inside that post has to be backed by. No instrument
prescribes the wording.

### What this file does not cover

Điều 56.1 loads eight duties on `người kinh doanh dịch vụ quảng cáo trên mạng` at 20 to 30 million,
running from contact notification and verifying the advertiser's business registration through record
retention, technical controls on unlawful ad products, algorithm disclosure, a consumer complaints
channel, platform transparency and periodic reporting. Those bind an agency or a platform rather than a
brand's communications team, so they are named here and left out of the table.

Điều 58 on broadcast is unread. So is press law, which is where `bản tin`, the permitted format of a supplement, and whether a
wrap counts as a cover are actually settled. Editorial norms around gifts, press trips and embargoes
are not law at all.
