## Research protocol

Use sources to learn structures, market signals, and execution standards. Do not trace, clone, or closely imitate a specific campaign, layout, photograph, or artist style.

1. Search the audience problem, product mechanism, physical materials, and channel separately.
2. Capture at least three sources from different categories.
3. Record the useful pattern and the saturated pattern.
4. Build an original concept from the brief rather than averaging the references.
5. Re-check URLs and platform requirements when executing; access and specifications can change.
