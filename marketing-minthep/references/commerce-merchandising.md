# Commerce and Merchandising

## Commerce decision sequence

Build PDP, marketplace, catalog, and retail assets to answer:

1. What exactly is it?
2. Is it relevant to my situation?
3. Why is this version worth choosing?
4. What evidence reduces risk?
5. Which variant, size, bundle, or plan is right?
6. What is included, delivered, or required?
7. What happens after purchase?

Do not treat ecommerce as a campaign poster plus a buy button.

## Minimum media sequence

Adapt order to category and platform:

1. Main product recognition frame.
2. Alternate angles or interface states.
3. Scale, fit, capacity, or room context.
4. Material, texture, construction, or detail macro.
5. Product in use with a believable hand/body/environment.
6. Mechanism or feature proof.
7. Variant, collection, or selection guide.
8. Bundle and what's included.
9. Instructions, care, compatibility, or dimensions.
10. Trust/proof asset when substantiated.

Use `assets/registries/asset-formats.json` instead of inventing a new shot list each time.

## Listing copy contract

Create only from confirmed truth:

- Search-aware title without keyword stuffing.
- One-line value proposition.
- Benefit bullets tied to mechanism or specification.
- Product description and use context.
- Variant, size, material, compatibility, care, and included-items data.
- FAQ and objection handling.
- Shipping, returns, warranty, price, availability, and promotion only when confirmed.
- Alt text and captions that describe the actual image.

## Marketplace and retailer adaptation

- Read current marketplace image, text, prohibited-claim, and category requirements before export.
- Keep a clean source master and placement-specific derivatives.
- Separate platform-required main image from editorial/lifestyle secondary images.
- Keep SKU names, color, scale, labels, and included accessories consistent across catalog, ads, and PDP.
- Treat Amazon/DSP/Stores, Google Merchant, retailer media, and local marketplaces as distinct placements.

## Merchandising system

For SKU families record:

- Parent/child relationship.
- Variant axis: color, flavor, size, finish, plan, bundle, or compatibility.
- Shared camera, background, crop, scale, and shadow rules.
- Which differences must be immediately visible at thumbnail size.
- Bundle logic and cross-sell/upsell relationship.
- Seasonal refresh rules without changing product truth.

## Return-reduction QA

Reject when assets hide or misrepresent:

- Size, fit, material, color, finish, capacity, compatibility, assembly, contents, or care.
- Product limitations or required accessories.
- Label, ingredient, safety, warranty, shipping, or return information.
- Perspective that makes a product appear larger or slimmer than it is.
- Lifestyle props that look included but are not.

Measure listing CTR, media engagement, add-to-cart, PDP conversion, AOV, variant selection, and return reasons. Do not optimize CTR by increasing ambiguity.
