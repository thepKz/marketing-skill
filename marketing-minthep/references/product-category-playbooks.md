# Product and Business-Model Playbooks

Use after selecting the primary workbench. These are proof and asset overlays, not complete strategies.

## Beauty and personal care

Prioritize exact pack/shade, texture/application, routine fit, skin/material behavior, ingredient/mechanism evidence, creator demonstration, and compliant claims. Useful assets: packshot set, swatch/texture, application macro, routine, shade/variant guide, ingredient/process proof, UGC, campaign beauty image. Reject plastic skin, fake clinical diagrams, unsupported before/after, or invented ingredients.

## Fashion, footwear, and accessories

Prioritize fit, silhouette, construction, fabric movement, size, detail, styling range, care, and provenance when confirmed. Useful assets: clean front/back, on-body fit, movement, detail macro, scale, styling combinations, size guide, collection lineup, editorial campaign. Reject lens-made proportions, cloned models, or hidden fit limitations.

## Jewelry and luxury goods

Prioritize material, finish, scale on body, closure, craft, provenance, packaging, service, and authenticity evidence. Use controlled reflection, macro, on-body scale, packaging/unboxing, craft/process, editorial still life, and press-ready imagery. Never invent gemstone, metal, origin, rarity, or certification.

## Food, beverage, and CPG

Prioritize pack recognition, appetite, texture, preparation, serving, occasion, ingredients, shelf behavior, trial, and repeat purchase. Use hero pack, real serving, process, ingredient/material, scale, multipack, retail/POS, delivery-app thumbnail, and seasonal assets. Reject fake steam, impossible splashes, or misleading serving size.

## Electronics and appliances

Prioritize exact device geometry, ports, interface, dimensions, setup, compatibility, use case, performance evidence, included items, and support/warranty when confirmed. Use clean multi-angle set, interface capture, scale, installation, feature demo, comparison, accessories, environment, and explainer. Do not generate fake UI or specifications.

## Home, furniture, and decor

Prioritize dimensions, room scale, materials, assembly, care, storage/capacity, color, and delivery. Use cutout, room context, scale, material macro, alternate configuration, dimensions, assembly, collection lineup, and seasonal room stories. Prevent wide-angle distortion and misleading room size.

## SaaS, apps, and digital products

Prioritize real workflow, interface artifacts, time/risk change, collaboration, integration, onboarding, security/compliance evidence, and customer outcomes. Use actual screenshots, annotated flows, demo video, before/after process, case study, comparison, webinar, founder/expert POV, sales deck, and lifecycle onboarding. Never generate a fake product UI as proof.

## Services and local businesses

Prioritize service packaging, process, people, tools, location, qualification, response expectations, case evidence, reviews with permission, booking/quote path, and local trust. Use real staff/site/tools, process sequence, completed work, FAQ, service comparison, local landing pages, Google Business Profile-compatible media, lead form, and follow-up. Reject generic stock teams and fake badges.

## Hospitality, travel, and experiences

Prioritize place, room/spatial truth, amenities, service, arrival, food, local context, packages, seasonality, availability, and booking terms. Use room/venue sequence, map/context, service moment, itinerary, event, UGC, OTA/direct-booking derivatives, and press gallery. Avoid generic destination stereotypes and misleading room perspectives.

## Education, knowledge products, and personal brands

Prioritize curriculum/artifact, teaching method, expertise evidence, sample lesson, learner work, community behavior, fit, time commitment, and verified outcomes. Use annotated material, lesson clips, founder/expert content, syllabus, case study, webinar, lead magnet, launch, sales page, and lifecycle nurture. Reject fake income, enrollment, or transformation claims.

## Art, handmade, collectibles, and cultural products

Prioritize authorship, material, scale, process, edition/provenance, installation/use context, packaging, and story. Use clean documentation, macro, process, studio portrait, installation, editorial interpretation, collection/edition guide, press kit, and collector-facing sales material. Distinguish documentary images from conceptual campaign art.
