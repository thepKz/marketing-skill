# Market Assessment Contract

Answer whether a market is attractive enough for the stated objective, for whom, and under what conditions.

## Required sections

- Executive conclusion and confidence.
- Category definition in customer language.
- Demand signals and their bias.
- Bottom-up low/base/high sizing with visible arithmetic.
- Direct, indirect, substitute, and do-nothing alternatives.
- Competitor comparison on switching-relevant axes.
- Price ladder with observed sources.
- Positioning whitespace and why it may be empty.
- Regulation, supply, seasonality, and platform risks.
- What evidence would change the recommendation.

Numbers without a URL/date are `inferred` or `unknown`, never `confirmed`.
