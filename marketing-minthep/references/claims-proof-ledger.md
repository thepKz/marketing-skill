# Claims and Proof Ledger

Create a ledger before public-facing copy, ads, listings, PR, creator content, or sales collateral when claims matter.

## Required fields

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `claim_id` | Stable identifier used by assets and copy |
| `claim_text` | Exact approved wording or bounded paraphrase |
| `claim_type` | Product fact, performance, health, environmental, comparative, testimonial, price, offer, certification |
| `evidence_source` | Document, test, product spec, customer approval, public source |
| `owner` | Person responsible for accuracy |
| `market/channel` | Where the claim is approved |
| `disclaimer` | Required qualification or disclosure |
| `expires/review_date` | When evidence or approval must be rechecked |
| `status` | Draft, approved, rejected, expired |

Unknown or unapproved claims must not appear in generation prompts, image labels, packaging, testimonials, press quotes, or public copy.

## Proof hierarchy

Prefer direct demonstration and product evidence, then customer/expert evidence, data, process, or credible proxy. Match proof to the decision and disclose limitations.

For endorsements, disclose material connections clearly and near the endorsement. A public social profile, press mention, celebrity reference, or generated lookalike does not create authorization or endorsement.
