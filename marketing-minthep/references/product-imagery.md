## Product fidelity first

Request a clear product reference when the packaging, logo, color, shape, label, or finish must be exact. Create a lock list before prompting:

- Silhouette and proportions.
- Logo placement and orientation.
- Packaging color and material.
- Cap, pump, closure, seams, and transparent parts.
- Required label text and prohibited invented text.
- Surface condition: new, used, wet, chilled, textured, matte, glossy, metallic, translucent.

Without a reference, describe the image as a concept and avoid readable microtext, certifications, ingredients, or claims.

## Composition families

Choose one family deliberately. Do not mix every technique into one frame.

### Standard: clear and dependable

1. **Hero packshot**: centered or optically centered, strong silhouette, controlled shadow, immediate recognition.
2. **Three-quarter pedestal**: depth, label visibility, material response, moderate negative space.
3. **In-context use**: product shown at the exact moment it creates value.
4. **Benefit demonstration**: mechanism or before/after evidence without deceptive exaggeration.
5. **Range architecture**: consistent scale and spacing across variants or SKUs.

Use standard compositions for ecommerce, launch clarity, catalogs, retail, and conversion assets.

### Distinctive: controlled rule breaks

1. **Crop intrusion**: let the product enter from an edge while preserving recognition and label hierarchy.
2. **Material tension**: contrast the product with one unexpected but meaningful material.
3. **Negative-space inversion**: place the product in the smaller field and let copy or environment carry scale.
4. **Macro-to-whole**: pair a tactile detail with a smaller complete packshot.
5. **Kinetic freeze**: motion, splash, powder, fabric, or ingredient behavior that demonstrates a real benefit.
6. **Off-axis system**: use a deliberate diagonal or asymmetric balance that survives channel crops.
7. **Scale disruption**: enlarge or miniaturize only when the metaphor supports the campaign idea.

Before rendering an unusual move, finish this sentence in one line: "This image is strange in *this*
specific way because the product does *this*." If the strangeness could be swapped for a different
strangeness without changing that sentence, it is decoration and the concept is still missing.
A floating serum bottle ringed by orbiting citrus is arbitrary; the same bottle suspended in a
cross-section of the gel it disperses into is the mechanism made visible.

### Corporate: proof-led confidence

1. Product with technical callouts and restrained evidence.
2. Modular system showing process, components, or lifecycle.
3. Operator or customer context with the product as a working tool.
4. Case-study composition: situation, product action, measurable outcome.
5. Industrial or architectural framing with precise scale and materials.

Avoid generic blue gradients, glowing data lines, fake dashboards, handshakes, and anonymous glass offices.

### Ecommerce set

Produce a coherent set when relevant:

- Front or primary hero.
- Three-quarter view.
- Back or information side.
- Scale-in-hand or scale reference.
- Texture or material macro.
- In-use moment.
- Included parts or bundle.
- Variant or range comparison.

## Camera and light decisions

Specify choices that support the product:

- Lens behavior: wide environmental, normal truthful, short telephoto compression, macro detail.
- Camera height and angle.
- Key light size, direction, hardness, and color.
- Fill level and shadow density.
- Surface and background reflectance.
- Polarized or controlled reflections for glossy products when appropriate.
- Contact shadow and weight; products must not float unintentionally.

Avoid generic phrases such as "cinematic lighting" without describing the actual light behavior.

## Typography-safe composition

When the image will carry copy:

- Reserve one explicit text field with low visual entropy.
- Keep the product and important features outside likely crop and UI zones.
- Define left-copy, right-copy, top-copy, or no-copy variants.
- Generate separate compositions for `9:16`, `4:5`, `1:1`, and wide web rather than cropping one master blindly.
- Do not ask the image model to render final legal or packaging copy. Add typography in design software when fidelity matters.

## Product prompt additions

Include:

- Product reference priority and lock list.
- Exact physical materials and finish.
- Product scale and orientation.
- Composition family and focal hierarchy.
- Environment, surface, props, and their narrative reason.
- Camera and light behavior.
- Crop and text-safe field.
- Negative constraints: no deformation, duplicate parts, floating, fake labels, random props, inconsistent reflections, or impossible shadows.
