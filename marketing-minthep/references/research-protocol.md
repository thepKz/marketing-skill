# Research Protocol

Use this protocol whenever the user asks for a plan, market assessment, competitor analysis, or feasibility judgment.

## Research loop

1. Convert the brief into decision questions: demand, audience, alternatives, willingness to pay, channel access, and constraints.
2. Assign each question a source tier: first-party/official, primary customer evidence, reputable secondary research, or directional discovery.
3. Search in the user's market and language first; record query, source URL, publisher, publication date, retrieval date, and the exact supported claim.
4. Triangulate important conclusions with at least three independent signals. Separate observed facts from inference.
5. Stop when the decision threshold is met, not when the search feels exhaustive. State what remains unknown and the evidence that would change the recommendation.

For scientific, health, behavioral, environmental, or efficacy claims, prefer systematic reviews/meta-analyses, primary peer-reviewed studies, recognized standards, and official public-health/regulatory sources. Record population, method, sample size, outcome, effect size when available, limitations, and whether the evidence supports correlation or causation. A blog that cites a study is a discovery source, not the evidence source.

## Evidence ledger

Every finding must have: `finding_id`, question, source, date, quote/data point, interpretation, confidence (`high|medium|low`), bias/limitation, and allowed use. Never turn a directional search result into a market-size fact.

## Deliverables

For a non-marketer, explain the conclusion before the jargon. Produce a Markdown report with an executive answer, evidence table, assumptions, market/competitor implications, recommended decision, risks, and next research actions. Cite URLs inline and include a source appendix.

## Safety

Do not invent TAM/SAM/SOM, customer quotes, competitor pricing, regulations, or trend claims. Use ranges and show arithmetic for bottom-up sizing. If browsing is unavailable, return a research plan and clearly mark the report as unverified.
