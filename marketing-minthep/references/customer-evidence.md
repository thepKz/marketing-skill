# Customer Evidence

Use public reviews, forums, search language, interviews, surveys, support logs, and sales notes only when their provenance is recorded.

Capture verbatim language with source and date, then code it into situation, desired progress, trigger, objection, workaround, and buying criterion. Distinguish frequency from intensity. Do not manufacture personas or quotes. End with one beachhead segment, its job-to-be-done, awareness stage, and the evidence gap to validate next.
