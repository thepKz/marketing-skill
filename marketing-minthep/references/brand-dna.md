# Brand DNA

## Purpose

Create a durable `BRAND.md` that prevents every campaign from starting at zero. Capture decisions, not generic adjectives. Keep unknowns explicit and never turn an inference into a brand fact.

## Extraction workflow

1. Read existing brand guides, product pages, campaigns, packaging, social profiles, and design tokens.
2. Separate stable identity from campaign-specific styling.
3. Ask only about conflicts that materially change positioning, casting, claims, or design.
4. Write confirmed facts first, then label inferred defaults.
5. Record the evidence path or URL for consequential decisions.

## BRAND.md contract

```markdown
# Brand

## Positioning
[Audience, category, unique mechanism, desired belief.]

## Product Truth
- Confirmed products, features, materials, ingredients, price, and proof.
- Prohibited or unsupported claims.

## Audience
- Primary audience, awareness, context, objections, language, market.

## Voice
- Three physical or behavioral voice words.
- Sentence rhythm, vocabulary, CTA posture, prohibited phrases.

## Visual Grammar
- Color strategy, materiality, typography character, composition, image behavior.
- One dependable pattern and one permitted departure pattern.

## Product Locks
- Geometry, logo, packaging, color, label, finish, proportions, legal copy.

## Human Direction
- Adult casting rules, styling, makeup, body diversity, realism, consent limits.

## Proof Rules
- Approved claim sources, testimonials, demonstrations, data, disclaimers.

## Cultural Context
- Market, language, symbols, sensitivities, localization notes.

## Anti-references
- Category reflexes, visual tropes, phrases, campaign devices, competitor similarities.

## Providers and Production
- Preferred image providers, prompt language, reference priority, export requirements.

## Evidence
- Source path or URL, observation date, what it proves.
```

## Voice fingerprint

Define voice through contrasts:

- Direct vs exploratory.
- Clinical vs sensory.
- Expert vs peer.
- Compressed vs narrative.
- Calm vs urgent.
- Formal vs conversational.

Place the brand on each axis, then write the same sentence twice — once on-voice, once in the
version a competitor would write. If the two are hard to tell apart, the axis positions are
decorative and the voice is not defined yet.

Ban `elevate`, `unlock`, `revolutionize`, and `all-in-one`. They sit at no position on any axis
above, which is exactly the problem: a word that survives every setting describes none of them.
See `copywriting.md` for what to replace each one with. If the brand already uses one of these in
market, record it as an inherited term with the date it was adopted, so the next writer knows it is
a legacy constraint rather than a house style worth extending.

## Visual fingerprint

Record:

- Dominant subject behavior: product, human, process, proof, place, or typography.
- Camera distance, crop tendency, light behavior, texture, and imperfection level.
- Geometry: hard seams, soft fields, asymmetry, repetition, scale, or negative space.
- Color commitment: restrained, committed, full palette, or drenched.
- Product-to-human balance and channel crop rules.
- AI tells explicitly forbidden for this brand.

## Update policy

- Preserve confirmed identity by default.
- Add campaign learnings only after evidence repeats across meaningful tests.
- Record a date and reason for each material change.
- Keep campaign experiments outside stable brand rules until validated.
