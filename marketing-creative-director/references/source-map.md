## Research protocol

Use sources to learn structures, market signals, and execution standards. Do not trace, clone, or closely imitate a specific campaign, layout, photograph, or artist style.

1. Search the audience problem, product mechanism, physical materials, and channel separately.
2. Capture at least three sources from different categories.
3. Record the useful pattern and the saturated pattern.
4. Build an original concept from the brief rather than averaging the references.
5. Re-check URLs and platform requirements when executing; access and specifications can change.

## Product and packaging references

| Source | URL | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| The Dieline | https://thedieline.com/ | Packaging systems, material choices, shelf behavior |
| Packaging of the World | https://www.packagingoftheworld.com/ | Product presentation, packaging categories, mockup patterns |
| Pinterest Trends | https://trends.pinterest.com/ | Product styling demand and seasonal visual language |

Study physical constraints: material reflectance, label hierarchy, closure, scale, use context, and shelf distance. Do not add packaging claims or details not present in product references.
