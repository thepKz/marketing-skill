# Industry Playbooks

Use these as starting constraints, never as aesthetic recipes. Product truth and audience context override category habits.

## Beauty and K-beauty

- Core proofs: texture, application, ingredient mechanism, routine fit, verified testing, real skin behavior.
- Useful scenes: bathroom window, backstage touch-up, commute, desk, dance practice, real vanity.
- Reject: plastic glass skin, petals, water splash, pastel pedestal, fake clinical graphics, impossible V-line faces.
- Human default: adult, RAW iPhone quality, Korean K-pop-inspired makeup, realistic skin, plausible slender anatomy when no direction conflicts.

## Fashion and accessories

- Core proofs: silhouette, construction, fabric movement, fit, styling range, detail, provenance.
- Useful scenes: fitting room, transit, venue arrival, workroom, street interaction, material macro.
- Reject: empty luxury haze, random marble, cloned models, stiff catalog poses used for every asset.

## Food, beverage, and CPG

- Core proofs: appetite, texture, preparation, ingredient, pack recognition, occasion, shelf behavior.
- Useful scenes: hands in process, imperfect table, condensation, real kitchen or store light.
- Reject: floating ingredients, impossible splashes, fake steam, over-clean surfaces, unreadable labels.

## SaaS and B2B

- Core proofs: workflow change, artifact created, time saved, risk reduced, integration, operator confidence.
- Useful scenes: actual interface sequence, before/after process, annotated artifact, customer context.
- Reject: purple glow, floating nodes, fake dashboards, unreadable hero screenshot, generic office handshake.

## Ecommerce and retail

- Core proofs: product clarity, scale, comparison, use, variant range, shipping, return confidence.
- Build: hero packshot, three-quarter, scale-in-hand, macro, in-use, bundle, comparison, channel crops.
- Reject: one master image blindly cropped everywhere, fake reviews, misleading scarcity.

## Hospitality and travel

- Core proofs: place, light, service moment, spatial flow, local detail, arrival and stay sequence.
- Use one decisive photograph or scene per major promise.
- Reject: generic infinity pool, beige luxury, empty lobby, copied destination stereotypes.

## Wellness and fitness

- Core proofs: routine adherence, coaching method, equipment, recovery, measurable but supported outcomes.
- Use real effort, rest, environment, and body diversity.
- Reject: beige serenity default, unsupported transformation, impossible bodies, pseudo-medical claims.

## Local services and professional firms

- Core proofs: process transparency, local knowledge, specialist judgment, response time, case evidence.
- Use real tools, staff, site conditions, and completed work.
- Reject: generic stock teams, handshake imagery, fake badges, decorative city skylines.

## Creator, education, and knowledge products

- Core proofs: artifact quality, teaching method, transformation of understanding, curriculum, learner work.
- Use annotated notes, process frames, real outputs, and structured before/after understanding.
- Reject: laptop coffee stock, fake income claims, motivational quote graphics, empty community screenshots.

## Playbook selection test

Before committing, state:

1. The category reflex being rejected.
2. The product-specific mechanism replacing it.
3. The proof visible in the asset.
4. The channel behavior the idea supports.
